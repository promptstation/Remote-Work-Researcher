# Nonresponse, Bias, and Incentives

## Mission

Nonresponse bias = (nonresponse rate) × (difference between respondents and nonrespondents). A 10% RR can be less biased than a 60% RR if the missing are similar. Chase the difference, not the trophy RR.

## Core output

A **Nonresponse Note**: RR (AAPOR type), known differences, follow-up protocol, incentive, likely bias direction.

## Phase 1 — Rates

Use AAPOR definitions (RR1–RR6). Item vs unit nonresponse. Breakoffs on web.

## Phase 2 — Reduce vs adjust

Callbacks, mixed mode, incentives (prepaid often beat promised), shorter instruments. Then weighting (next skill). Do not pretend a low RR with no follow-up is "fine because we weighted."

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Report RR correctly.
2. Compare respondents to frame on known vars.
3. Protocol to raise RR among likely-different groups.
4. Direction of remaining bias.

## Anti-patterns

- RR as quality
- No AAPOR definition
- Incentives that buy only easy respondents
- Ignoring item missing on income/vote

## Validation gate

AAPOR-style RR; bias not assumed zero; follow-up plan.

## Final principle

The people you did not hear from are part of the estimate. If they differ, your percentage is about respondents.
