# Total Survey Error and Study Purpose

## Mission

A survey is an inference from answers some people gave to a questionnaire. Error comes from who is missing and from what was asked. Sample size is one piece. TSE is the map.

## Core output

A **Survey Spec**: purpose, target population, main estimand, dominant error risks, design sketch.

## Phase 1 — Purpose

Descriptive prevalence, comparison of groups, change over time, or input to a model. Each wants a different error budget (trend needs stable measurement more than a tiny SE).

## Phase 2 — TSE

Coverage, sampling, nonresponse, measurement, processing. Rank which would flip the decision. Spend money there.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

Write purpose and estimand. Rank errors. Then sampling and questionnaire skills.

## Anti-patterns

- n=1000 as quality
- Ignoring measurement
- Buying a panel without a purpose

## Validation gate

Purpose; estimand; ranked error sources.

## Final principle

If you cannot name the error that would change your mind, you are shopping for a sample size.
