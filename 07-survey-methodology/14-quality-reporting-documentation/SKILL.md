# Quality Reporting and Documentation

## Mission

Undocumented surveys cannot be interpreted. A methods statement is part of the estimate. If a vendor will not disclose, the number is not research-grade.

## Core output

A **Methods Statement**: sponsor, target, frame, design, n, field dates, mode, RR (AAPOR), weighting, questionnaire source, known errors.

## Phase 1 — Minimum disclosure

Who paid, who was eligible, how selected, how many, when, how asked, how weighted, what RR, what the questions were. Missing any of these → do not treat as a high-quality estimate.

## Phase 2 — Workflow

Write the statement before the press release. The finding cites it.

## Anti-patterns

- "n=2000 nationally representative" as the whole method
- Hidden sponsor
- No questionnaire
- No RR

## Validation gate

The minimum list is complete or gaps are explicit.

## Final principle

If another shop could not rerun or critique the design from your documentation, you published a number, not a survey.
