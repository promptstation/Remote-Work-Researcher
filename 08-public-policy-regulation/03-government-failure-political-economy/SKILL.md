# Government Failure, Political Economy, and Institutions

## Mission

Governments fail too: capture, career incentives, short horizons, information, coordination. A rationale for intervention is necessary, not sufficient. Design for the government you have.

## Core output

A **Political-Economy Note**: veto players, concentrated vs diffuse interests, agency incentives, likely distortion, design responses.

## Phase 1 — Players

Who gains, who pays, who organises. Concentrated incumbents beat diffuse consumers/workers unless institutions compensate.

## Phase 2 — Agencies

Budget, revolving doors, output metrics that are not outcomes (inspections, not harm). Time inconsistency: tomorrow's minister undoes today's rule.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Winners/losers.
2. Vetoes.
3. Agency metric.
4. How the paper policy will be bent; how to make it harder to bend.

## Anti-patterns

- Benevolent planner
- Ignoring concentrated losers
- Reform that needs capacity the agency does not have

## Validation gate

Players named; distortion predicted; not only "political will."

## Final principle

Policies are implemented by people with careers. If your design needs saints, it is not a design.
