# Labour, Employment, and Social Regulation (Policy Side)

## Mission

Employment rules are policies: they have rationales, instruments, delivery, and politics. Use labour-economics for incidence (Course 1 §13, Course 6). Use this skill for the policy object: who makes it, who enforces it, who is outside it (informal).

## Core output

A **Labour-Rule Policy Brief**: problem, rationale, instrument, coverage (who is outside), enforcement, politics, economic incidence pointer.

## Phase 1 — Coverage

Employees vs self-employed vs informal. A beautiful EPL that covers 20% of workers is a dualism machine unless designed otherwise.

## Phase 2 — Process

Minima, EPL, UI, discrimination, leave, OSH — different agencies, different vetoes, different data.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Problem and rationale.
2. Coverage map.
3. Instrument + enforcement.
4. Incidence (cite labour skills).
5. Political-economy.

## Anti-patterns

- Elasticity without coverage
- Copying OECD labour law into high-informality settings
- No inspectorate

## Validation gate

Coverage; enforcement; economics linked, not duplicated as slogans.

## Final principle

Labour regulation is a policy that some workers never meet. Write who is outside the law, or you wrote a formal-sector fairy tale.
