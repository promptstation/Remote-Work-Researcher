# Ethics, Rights, and Distribution in Policy

## Mission

Net benefits can hide concentrated harm. Rights can rule out some instruments regardless of CBA. Targeting can stigmatise; universalism can leak to the rich. Put these on the page as constraints, not as afterthoughts.

## Core output

A **Normative Constraints Note**: rights issues, distribution table, targeting vs universal trade-off, whose welfare is in the objective.

## Phase 1 — Rights

Bodily integrity, due process, non-discrimination, privacy. If an instrument violates a right the polity treats as binding, say so and pick another instrument. This skill does not adjudicate law.

## Phase 2 — Distribution

Winners/losers by income, region, sex, formality. A regressive efficient tax needs a transfer if equity is in the mandate.

## Phase 3 — Targeting

Errors of exclusion vs inclusion. Informal workers often excluded. Universal benefits cost more and leak; targeted benefits miss.

## Phase 4 — Workflow

1. Who is in the social welfare function.
2. Rights screen.
3. Distribution table.
4. Rec that survives the screen.

## Anti-patterns

- NPV as ethics
- Ignoring exclusion of the informal
- Rights as decoration

## Validation gate

Distribution table; rights screen; targeting errors.

## Final principle

A policy that is efficient on average can be unjust in its incidence. If you do not show incidence, you have not finished the ethics.
