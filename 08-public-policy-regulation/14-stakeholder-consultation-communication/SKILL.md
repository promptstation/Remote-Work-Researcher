# Stakeholder Analysis, Consultation, and Communication

## Mission

Veto players and noisy losers shape outcomes. Consultation can inform or can be captured. Briefs are BLUF: decision, options, recommendation, distribution, risks.

## Core output

A **Stakeholder Map + Brief**: players, interests, power, consultation design, 2-page brief.

## Phase 1 — Map

Interest × power. Quiet winners, loud losers. Who can litigate, strike, or fund opposition.

## Phase 2 — Consultation

Open enough to learn; structured enough not to be a veto. Publish comments. Do not confuse a roundtable of incumbents with the public.

## Phase 3 — Brief shape

Decision, 2–3 options, rec, costs/benefits, distribution, implementation, risks, annex evidence.

## Anti-patterns

- 80-page first product
- Consultation as incumbents only
- Hiding losers

## Validation gate

Map; BLUF brief; losers named.

## Final principle

Policy communication is a decision memo, not a manifesto. If the minister cannot choose from it, rewrite.
