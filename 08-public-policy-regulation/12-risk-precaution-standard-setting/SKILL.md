# Risk, Precaution, and Standard-Setting

## Mission

Risk assessment estimates magnitudes and uncertainties. Risk management chooses a standard given values, cost, and distribution. Precaution is a stance under uncertainty, not a free "ban it." Zero risk is rarely a policy.

## Core output

A **Standard Memo**: hazard, assessment (with uncertainty), options, who bears residual risk, chosen rule and why.

## Phase 1 — Assessment

Dose-response, exposure, vulnerable groups, uncertainty. Do not launder value choices into the science chapter.

## Phase 2 — Management

ALARA, de minimis, cost-risk, rights-based bans. State the choice. Compare residual risks to other tolerated risks if that helps, without whataboutism.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Science + uncertainty.
2. Options including do-nothing.
3. Distribution of residual risk.
4. Decision.

## Anti-patterns

- Zero-risk
- Precaution as unstructured panic
- Hiding values in "the science says"

## Validation gate

Uncertainty explicit; values explicit; residual risk named.

## Final principle

Standards are value judgments under uncertainty. Keep the judgment visible.
