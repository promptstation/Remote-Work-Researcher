# Market Failure, Public Goods, and Rationales for Intervention

## Mission

Intervention needs a rationale: efficiency (a named market failure) and/or equity (a named distributional goal) and/or rights. "Something should be done" is not a rationale. Neither is "other countries do it."

## Core output

A **Rationale Note**: failure or equity goal, evidence it exists here, why private bargaining will not fix it, what success looks like.

## Phase 1 — Efficiency catalogue

Externalities, public goods, asymmetric information, market power, missing markets, behavioural (use carefully). Name the missing price.

## Phase 2 — Equity

Who is harmed under the status quo, who would pay under intervention. Equity is allowed; it should not wear an efficiency costume.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Rationale type.
2. Evidence.
3. If none, recommend non-intervention or a different problem definition.
4. Then pick an instrument.

## Anti-patterns

- Rationale after the instrument
- Everything as an externality
- Equity hidden as "market failure"
- Copying OECD as rationale

## Validation gate

Named failure or equity goal; evidence; instrument not yet locked.

## Final principle

If you cannot say which price is missing or which inequality you are targeting, you are not ready to regulate.
