# Comparative Regulation and Policy Transfer

## Mission

Policies sit in legal families, state capacity, informal sectors, and political coalitions. Copying a Danish labour rule into a dual labour market is not transfer. It is costume.

## Core output

A **Transfer Note**: source policy, necessary conditions at origin, which are missing here, adapted design or "do not transfer."

## Phase 1 — Conditions

Capacity, coverage of the formal sector, unions, courts, data, fiscal space, legitimacy. List what made it work there.

## Phase 2 — Adaptation

Maybe the goal transfers and the instrument does not. Maybe a pilot. Maybe a different instrument with the same rationale.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. What worked, for whom, where.
2. Condition list.
3. Fit.
4. Adapt or refuse.

## Anti-patterns

- League-table policy shopping
- "Best practice" without conditions
- Ignoring informality and capacity

## Validation gate

Origin conditions; local gaps; adaptation not photocopy.

## Final principle

A policy is not a file you download. Transfer the mechanism, or do not transfer.
