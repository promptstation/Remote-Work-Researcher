# Law, Rules, and Regulatory Process

## Mission

A tweet is not a statute. Statutes, delegated rules, guidance, and circulars have different force and processes. Map the path and the vetoes in the relevant jurisdiction (as described by the user or public process). This is not legal advice.

## Core output

A **Process Map**: instrument type, maker, consultation, vetoes, timeline, enforcement body.

## Phase 1 — Hierarchy

Constitution/rights → statute → regulation → guidance. Guidance is easier and weaker. If you need a duty, you may need a statute.

## Phase 2 — Process

Consultation can be genuine or theatre. Impact assessments can bind or be box-ticking. Courts and international commitments can constrain.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. What legal force is needed?
2. Who can issue it?
3. Steps and time.
4. Who can sue or stall?

## Anti-patterns

- Policy memo as law
- Ignoring secondary legislation
- No enforcement body

## Validation gate

Instrument type; maker; not legal advice labelled.

## Final principle

If you cannot say which legal object you are changing, you are campaigning, not regulating.
