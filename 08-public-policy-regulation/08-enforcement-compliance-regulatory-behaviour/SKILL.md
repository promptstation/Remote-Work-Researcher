# Enforcement, Compliance, and Regulatory Behaviour

## Mission

Compliance = f(detection, sanction, norms, ability to comply). A fine nobody will face is a suggestion. In informal markets, enforcement can push activity further out of view.

## Core output

An **Enforcement ToC**: who must comply, how they are found, what happens, likely behavioural response.

## Phase 1 — Detection

Inspectors, data trails, complaints, third-party. Coverage and targeting. Random vs risk-based.

## Phase 2 — Response

Deterrence vs advice-and-repair. Repeat players vs one-shot. Penalties that bankrupt vs that are a fee.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Who is the regulatee (formal firm, informal, individual)?
2. Detection.
3. Sanction and support.
4. Displacement risk.

## Anti-patterns

- Fine inflation without detection
- Assuming 100% compliance
- Ignoring informal displacement

## Validation gate

Detection named; regulatee type; displacement considered.

## Final principle

Unenforced law is a different policy from enforced law. Analyse the one that will exist.
