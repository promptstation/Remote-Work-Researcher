# Implementation, Delivery, and Administrative Capacity

## Mission

Policy is what is delivered. Street-level workers ration, IT systems fail, targeting lists are wrong, and informal clients never appear. Design the delivery chain or do not claim the outcome.

## Core output

A **Delivery Stress-Test**: chain, bottlenecks, targeting errors, IT/people capacity, likely implemented form.

## Phase 1 — Chain

Who does what to whom, with what information, in how many days. Count handoffs.

## Phase 2 — Failure modes

Cream-skimming, overload, corruption, exclusion of the undocumented/informal, last-mile. Pilot vs scale.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Draw the chain.
2. Capacity vs volume.
3. Who is excluded by the form.
4. Simplify or resource; do not add a new form if the old one is the bottleneck.

## Anti-patterns

- Law = outcome
- Unfunded mandates
- Targeting the easy-to-reach
- Digital-only in low-connectivity settings

## Validation gate

Chain drawn; bottleneck named; exclusion risk.

## Final principle

A policy that cannot be delivered is a press release. Write the delivery or withdraw the claim.
