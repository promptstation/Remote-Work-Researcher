# Geographic, Mobility, and Location Intelligence

## Mission

The market's geography is the catchment of people who would take the job, not the pin on the map. Commuting sheds, visa regimes, and remote policy redraw the market. Compare locations on supply, demand, pay, regulation, and risk — not on vibe.

## Core output

A **Location Comparison**: catchment definition, supply/demand/pay, constraints (visa, language, infrastructure), recommendation with the decision.

## Phase 1 — Catchment

On-site: commuting time, not metro administrative borders.  
Hybrid: days-in-office rule.  
Remote: legal employment entity + timezone + language, not "the world."

## Phase 2 — Compare

Use supply, demand, wage, and regulation skills. Add housing/amenity only as they affect reservation wages and attrition (urban skill for depth).

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Catchment rule.
2. Score locations on 4–6 criteria tied to the decision.
3. Sensitivity to remote policy.
4. Recommend; name the deal-breaker.

## Anti-patterns

- City GDP as labour supply
- Ignoring visas
- "Talent hub" as a slogan
- Remote as zero geography

## Validation gate

Catchment defined; multi-criteria; remote/visa addressed if relevant.

## Final principle

Location intelligence is catchment intelligence. Draw the catchment or do not rank the cities.
