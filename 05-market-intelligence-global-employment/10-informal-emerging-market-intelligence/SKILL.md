# Informal, Emerging-Market, and Hard-to-Measure Employment

## Mission

In many markets the modal job is informal or agricultural. Official LU1 and formal vacancy boards will lie. Use the developing-labour skill's indicators and say what you cannot know.

## Core output

A **Thin-Data Employment Brief**: what is known (status, sector, informal share), what is guessed, confidence, implication.

## Phase 1 — Hierarchy

LFS with informality modules > census > ILO modelled > admin formal > job boards (tiny slice) > anecdotes.

Never let a job board represent Nigeria's or India's labour market.

## Phase 2 — Methods when thin

Triangulate: sector output, tax registrations, mobile-money, qualitative employer interviews, import of comparable-country structure. Label expert judgment as such. Ranges, not false point estimates.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Modal job.
2. Official indicators that still work (EPR, sector).
3. Formal-only slice if the user hires formally — say it is a slice.
4. Confidence.

## Anti-patterns

- LinkedIn as the market
- LU1 as health
- Precise forecasts from modelled data
- Ignoring seasonality of rural work

## Validation gate

Informality addressed; source slice named; ranges if thin.

## Final principle

In a dual market, intelligence that only sees the formal island will confidently mislead.
