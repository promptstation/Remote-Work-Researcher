# Regulation, Policy, and Political Risk for Employment

## Mission

A cheap, plentiful labour market that you cannot legally access is not a market. Track immigration, localisation, wage floors, EPL, data-residency for remote work, and political risk as binding constraints. This is intelligence: "this rule exists and binds." Counsel confirms application.

## Core output

A **Constraint Scan**: jurisdiction, rules that bind this hire type, change watch, operational implication.

## Phase 1 — Binding rules

Visas and quotas, localisation percentages, professional licensing, minimum wages and pay reporting, EPL and termination cost, social contributions, entity requirements to employ, remote-work tax presence.

## Phase 2 — Political risk

Elections, strikes, sudden localisation decrees, conflict. Scenario, not panic. Horizon matched to the decision (a 3-month contractor vs a 10-year GCC).

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Hire type and jurisdiction.
2. Access (can we employ?).
3. Price (mandates).
4. Flexibility (EPL).
5. Watch items.

## Anti-patterns

- Cost-only location ranking
- Legal advice theatre
- Ignoring entity/tax presence for remote
- Static scans with no watchlist

## Validation gate

Jurisdiction named; hire type named; "not legal advice"; watch items.

## Final principle

Policy is part of the price and part of the quantity. If it is not in the brief, the brief is a travel brochure.
