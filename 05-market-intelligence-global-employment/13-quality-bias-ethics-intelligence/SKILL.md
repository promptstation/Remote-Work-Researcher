# Quality, Bias, and Ethics of Market Intelligence

## Mission

Intelligence fails by looking sure. Audit coverage, incentive of the source, and what you are not allowed to collect. Confidence language is mandatory.

## Core output

An **Intelligence QA**: coverage bias, concept mismatch, overclaim edits, ethical issues, confidence rating.

## Phase 1 — Bias catalogue

Platform users ≠ labour force. Posted jobs ≠ vacancies ≠ hires. Survey respondents ≠ nonrespondents. Modelled estimates ≠ official. Vendor "talent indices" with unpublished methods.

## Phase 2 — Ethics

No theft of competitor HRIS, no scraping behind login, no personal data of employees for "mapping" without basis. Aggregated public ads are different from personal profiles.

## Phase 3 — Language

Replace "shortage" with the evidence sentence. Replace "the market" with the slice. Grade confidence: high/medium/low and why.

## Phase 4 — Workflow

Run this skill on every brief before delivery.

## Anti-patterns

- Unsourced indices
- Personal data as CI
- False precision
- Hiding disagreement among sources

## Validation gate

Bias named; confidence graded; ethical path only.

## Final principle

The ethical and statistical limit is part of the product. Hide it and you have PR.
