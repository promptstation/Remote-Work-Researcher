# Continuous Monitoring Systems

## Mission

Markets move after the brief. A monitor is a small set of indicators with triggers that reopen the thesis — not a living museum of every series.

## Core output

A **Monitor Spec**: indicators, cadence, owners, triggers, what happens when fired.

## Phase 1 — Minimal set

For each live decision (this location, this occupation, this pay band): 4–8 indicators. Example: occupational employment, ads, wage growth, tightness, policy watch, competitor layoff news.

Freshness over completeness. A quarterly official series plus a monthly ad index beats 50 stale tiles.

## Phase 2 — Triggers

Pre-written: e.g. real posted wages +10% YoY and time-to-fill up → reopen pay band. Avoid dashboard theatre without actions.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Which theses are still live?
2. Minimal indicators.
3. Cadence and owner.
4. Trigger → review brief, do not auto-trade a labour market.

## Anti-patterns

- 40-series dashboards
- No owner
- No triggers
- Monitoring as a substitute for a decision

## Validation gate

Live theses named; small indicator set; triggers; owner.

## Final principle

Monitoring is attention with a tripwire. If nothing can trip, you are not monitoring. You are decorating a wall.
