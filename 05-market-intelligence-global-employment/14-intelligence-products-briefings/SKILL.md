# Intelligence Products and Briefings

## Mission

The brief answers the decision in the first ten lines: recommendation, why, confidence, what to watch. Charts earn those lines. Method sits in an annex.

## Core output

A **Decision Brief**: BLUF recommendation, 3–5 evidence bullets, confidence, watch/triggers, annex sources.

## Phase 1 — Shape

1. Decision restated  
2. Recommendation  
3. Evidence (supply, demand, price, constraint — only what moved the rec)  
4. Alternatives rejected  
5. Watch items  
6. Source annex

No mystery-novel structure. No 30-slide taxonomy lecture up front.

## Phase 2 — Style

Numbers with vintages. Verbs that match evidence. Maps and tables over paragraphs of adjectives ("vibrant ecosystem").

## Phase 3 — Workflow

Write the recommendation first. If evidence cannot support it, change the recommendation, not the adjectives.

## Anti-patterns

- Data dump
- Recommendation on the last slide
- Unsourced "hub" language
- Same brief for pay vs location vs policy decisions

## Validation gate

BLUF present; confidence present; watch items present; sources annexed.

## Final principle

Intelligence is a recommendation under uncertainty. If the reader cannot act, rewrite.
