# Employer, Competitor, and Talent-Competitor Intelligence

## Mission

Talent competitors are who hire the same people, not who sell the same product. A bank and a telco compete for data scientists. Track hiring velocity, layoffs, locations, and EVP signals legally from public traces.

## Core output

A **Talent-Competitor Brief**: competitor set, overlap roles, hiring/layoff pulse, locations, implications for pay and attrition.

## Phase 1 — Set

From occupation + location: who is posting, who employs large stocks, who is growing. Include public sector and GCCs when they hire the same occupation.

## Phase 2 — Signals

Job-ad volume (biased), announced hiring, layoffs, site openings/closures, visa filings where public, pay bands if disclosed. None is a full picture; triangulation.

Do not hack, dump, or use stolen employee lists. Public ads, filings, news, and permitted research only.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Overlap occupation.
2. Top competitors by demand.
3. Pulse: hiring vs cutting.
4. So-what for offer close-rates and attrition risk.

## Anti-patterns

- Product competitors only
- One leaked spreadsheet
- Treating ads as headcount
- Illegal CI

## Validation gate

Talent overlap defined; sources public; hiring vs ads distinguished.

## Final principle

You compete for people with whoever pays them to do similar tasks. Map that set, not the industry textbook.
