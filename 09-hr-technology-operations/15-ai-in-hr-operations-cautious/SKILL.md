# AI in HR Operations (Cautious)

## Mission

HR AI that affects hire, fire, or pay is a high-stakes model. Demand a job-related validity story, adverse-impact analysis, human review, logs, and a disable switch. A chatbot that drafts a letter is not the same as a ranking model. Do not let "AI" skip I-O and controls.

## Core output

An **HR-AI Gate**: use case, decision impact, evidence of validity, fairness, human-in-the-loop, data sent to vendor, go/no-go.

## Phase 1 — Classify

- Assistive (draft, schedule, search KB)
- Decision-shaping (rank, score, predict attrition, screen-out)

Decision-shaping inherits selection-system duties.

## Phase 2 — Tests

Job analysis link, local or transportable validity, adverse impact, stability, explainability to a candidate/auditor, retention of inputs, no scraping of extra PII, no vendor training on your data unless contracted.

Resume screeners that proxy for names/schools/gaps need extra scepticism.

## Phase 3 — Ops

Logs of scores, override reasons, fallback to structured non-AI process. Recertify models. Do not let the ATS silently turn on a ranking model.

## Phase 4 — Workflow

1. Impact class.
2. Evidence pack.
3. Legal/privacy review (counsel).
4. Pilot with human override.
5. No-go if evidence is a marketing PDF.

## Anti-patterns

- Vendor "bias-free" claims
- Screen-out without structured alternative
- Flight-risk scores to managers without a protocol
- Sending health/performance data to a model API

## Validation gate

Impact class; validity/fairness evidence for decision-shaping; human override; data terms.

## Final principle

If the model can reject a person, it is a selection device. Hold it to selection standards, or do not turn it on.
