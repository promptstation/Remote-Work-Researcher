# Learning, Performance, and Talent Systems (Ops View)

## Mission

Talent systems fail on adoption, data, and workflow, even when the I-O design is decent. Completions that nobody needs, 360s with no rater list, goals pasted once a year — operations problems. Do not confuse a content library with a training system.

## Core output

An **Ops Implementation**: workflows, required data, audiences, integrations to HRIS jobs, adoption plan, reports that match the I-O purpose.

## Phase 1 — LMS

Assignments tied to jobs (from HRIS), due dates, managers, completions as records. Optional libraries are search problems. Mandatory training needs exception and legal evidence.

## Phase 2 — PMS

Cycle calendar, who rates whom (org tree quality!), calibration workflow, visibility. If the org tree is wrong, appraisal routing is wrong.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Purpose from I-O skills.
2. Workflow in the tool.
3. HRIS integration for jobs/managers.
4. Adoption (managers first).
5. Data quality reports.

## Anti-patterns

- Content dump
- Go-live without org-tree hygiene
- Completions as learning
- 12 talent modules, 0 processes

## Validation gate

Workflow; HRIS job/manager feed; adoption; purpose not only software.

## Final principle

A talent module is a workflow with a record. If either is missing, you bought a shelf.
