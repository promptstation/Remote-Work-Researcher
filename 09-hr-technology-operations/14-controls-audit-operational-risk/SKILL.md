# Controls, Audit, and Operational Risk in HR

## Mission

HR/payroll risk: ghost employees, unauthorised pay, access after exit, silent data changes. Controls are preventive (SoD, approvals) and detective (logs, recon, recertification). Trust is not a control.

## Core output

A **Control Matrix**: risk, control, owner, evidence, frequency.

## Phase 1 — Risks

Ghosts, round-trip pay, bank-account takeover, terminated-with-access, privilege creep, incomplete leaver, GDPR dumps.

## Phase 2 — Controls

SoD (initiate ≠ approve ≠ pay). Audit logs on pay and bank fields. Joiner/leaver recon HRIS–IAM–payroll. Quarterly access recert. Surprise sampling of time and vendors.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Risk list for this org.
2. Matrix.
3. Evidence stored.
4. Test a sample.

## Anti-patterns

- Shared payroll logins
- No logs
- "We're too small for SoD" without compensating reviews
- Never recertifying access

## Validation gate

SoD or compensating; recon; logs on pay-sensitive fields.

## Final principle

If nobody could prove who changed a bank account, you do not have a payroll control environment.
