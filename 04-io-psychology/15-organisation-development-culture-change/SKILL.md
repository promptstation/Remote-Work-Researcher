# Organisation Development, Culture, and Change

## Mission

Culture is shared assumptions inferred from what is rewarded, punished, and ignored — not the values page. Climate is the shared perception of policies and practices. OD is planned change with diagnosis and evaluation. If you cannot diagnose, you cannot change.

## Core output

An **OD Diagnosis**: presenting problem, structure/process/climate/culture layer, data, intervention, evaluation design, likely resistance.

## Phase 1 — Layer

- **Structure:** spans, incentives, decision rights, information
- **Process:** how work actually flows
- **Climate:** shared perceptions (safety, service, justice)
- **Culture:** deeper assumptions (who may speak, what "good" is)

Values posters target none of these if incentives contradict them.

## Phase 2 — Diagnosis

Multiple methods: archival outcomes, climate survey, interviews, observation of meetings, incentive maps. Single-source "culture survey" is a climate snapshot at best.

Reorgs are structural interventions. They fail when they ignore informal networks and incentive remnants.

## Phase 3 — Change

Unfreeze–move–refreeze is a metaphor, not a plan. Plans need: sponsor with power, specific practices to stop/start, middle-manager capability, and a metric that could show failure.

Resistance is often information (the change is harmful to the work). Treat it as data.

## Phase 4 — Workflow

1. Presenting problem in outcomes.
2. Layer.
3. Evidence.
4. Smallest intervention that could work (practice, not slogan).
5. Evaluation and stop rule.

## Anti-patterns

- Culture as a brand campaign
- Reorg as default
- Survey without action
- Change with no metric
- Ignoring incentives

## Validation gate

Layer named; evidence not only slogans; intervention is a practice; evaluation present.

## Final principle

Organisations change when practices and payoffs change. Everything else is decoration on the old culture.
