# Performance Appraisal and Performance Management

## Mission

Appraisal is measurement plus a social system. It fails from bad criteria, untrained raters, conflicting purposes (develop vs fire vs pay), and forced distributions that invent differences. Fix purpose and criterion before buying software.

## Core output

A **PM Diagnosis or Design**: purpose(s), criteria, rating method, rater training, calibration, link to pay/development, failure modes.

## Phase 1 — Purpose conflict

Development, administrative (pay/promotion), and legal documentation want different honesty. Mixing them in one annual form produces safe middle ratings and surprise firings. Separate conversations if you can; if not, say which purpose wins when they conflict.

## Phase 2 — Formats

Behaviourally anchored / behavioural observation scales beat trait scales. 360s need rater source meaning (peer vs boss) and protection from popularity contests.

Forced ranking: creates competition in interdependent teams (personnel-economics skill). Use only if the organisation truly needs a relative ordering and can absorb the cooperation cost.

Goals: specific, accepted, with resources. Goals that ignore citizenship and safety will get what they measure.

## Phase 3 — Raters

Halo, leniency, recency, similar-to-me, opportunity bias. Frame-of-reference training beats a one-hour "avoid bias" slide.

Calibration meetings: useful if they challenge evidence; harmful if they just enforce a curve.

## Phase 4 — Workflow

1. Purpose.
2. Criteria from job analysis.
3. Format and sources.
4. Training and calibration rules.
5. Separate development from pay if possible.
6. Evaluate the system (reliability, user trust, downstream decisions).

## Anti-patterns

- Annual surprise
- Trait adjectives ("strategic," "passionate")
- Forced curve on a 6-person team
- 360 as a popularity score
- Software as the intervention

## Validation gate

Purpose named; behavioural criteria; rater training; purpose conflicts acknowledged.

## Final principle

Performance management is a measurement system inside politics. If it cannot be wrong on the evidence, it is only politics.
