# Recruitment, Selection, and Placement

## Mission

Selection is applied prediction under legal and fairness constraints. Combine a small number of job-relevant predictors, structure human judgment, and measure adverse impact. Unstructured interviews feel valid; they mostly are not.

## Core output

A **Selection System Design**: funnel, predictors, combination rule, cut policy, adverse-impact plan, candidate experience, evaluation.

## Phase 1 — Recruitment as the first predictor

Who applies sets the ceiling. Job-ad language, channels, and requirements (degree vs skill) change both quality and diversity of the pool. Tightening requirements is a selection decision.

## Phase 2 — Predictor methods (typical validity order, not a law)

Work samples / job simulations, structured interviews, cognitive tests, job knowledge, conscientiousness/integrity, situational judgment — then unstructured interviews and years-of-experience as weak defaults.

Structure interviews: same questions, anchored rating scales, trained interviewers, notes, IRR. "Culture chat" is an unstructured interview with extra bias.

## Phase 3 — Combine

- multiple hurdles vs compensatory (regression/unit-weighted)
- unit weights often beat estimated weights in small samples
- work samples late (cost) after cheap screens
- do not add predictors with no incremental validity

Cut scores: content-based or empirical; avoid arbitrary 80%. Banding and sliding bands are fairness tools, not magic.

## Phase 4 — Adverse impact and errors

Track pass rates by group. 80% rule is a heuristic, not a statistical test. Validity does not automatically legalise impact; jurisdictions differ — flag the need for counsel.

False positives vs false negatives: high-volume vs high-stakes roles need different thresholds.

## Phase 5 — Workflow

1. Job analysis → KSAOs.
2. Choose 2–4 predictors.
3. Structure the human parts.
4. Combination rule and cuts.
5. Adverse-impact simulation if data exist.
6. After hire, local validation against criteria.

## Anti-patterns

- Unstructured interviews as the system
- Degree requirements as a lazy GMA proxy
- Stacking five vendor tests
- Ignoring the applicant pool
- "We hire for potential" with no measure of potential

## Validation gate

Predictors job-linked; interviews structured; combination rule named; adverse impact addressed.

## Final principle

A selection system is a model that spends candidate time and legal risk. Spend them on predictors that earn validity.
