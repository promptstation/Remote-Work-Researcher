# Fairness, Justice, Diversity, and Inclusion at Work

## Mission

Justice is a psychological and organisational fact with performance and legal consequences. DEI work that never touches procedures (who is hired, rated, paid, heard) is messaging. Bias training alone is a weak intervention.

## Core output

A **Justice Review**: practice under review, distributive/procedural/interactional issues, adverse-impact or representation facts, intervention with evidence, evaluation.

## Phase 1 — Justice types

- **Distributive:** outcomes (pay, load, recognition) vs inputs
- **Procedural:** voice, consistency, accuracy, correctability, bias suppression
- **Interactional:** dignity in how decisions are explained

People accept hard outcomes better when procedures and explanations are sound — not infinitely.

## Phase 2 — DEI as system change

Representation and inclusion are downstream of recruitment channels, requirements, assessment, sponsorship, work design, and climate (harassment, psychological safety).

Evidence-leaning interventions: structured selection, diverse slates plus structured scoring, accountability for decisions, family-friendly job design, pay-process transparency. Evidence-weak as standalone: one-shot implicit-bias lectures, mandatory celebrating without procedure change.

Adverse impact analysis belongs on every high-stakes process (selection skill). Pay gaps: use the labour-discrimination skill for measurement; here, focus on the organisational process.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Which decisions (hire, rate, promote, pay, fire)?
2. Procedure map and data (rates, gaps).
3. Justice failure type.
4. Change the procedure; evaluate.
5. Training only as support, not as the intervention.

## Anti-patterns

- DEI as communications
- Bias workshop as the annual fix
- Ignoring harassment climate
- "Merit" as unstructured manager discretion
- Legal-only or PR-only frames

## Validation gate

Procedure named; data on outcomes; intervention not only training; justice type named.

## Final principle

Fairness is built in how decisions are made. If the procedure does not change, the poster did not work.
