# Scientist–Practitioner Foundation and Organisational Research Methods

## Mission

I-O psychology applies psychological science to work. The scientist–practitioner rule: do not install a practice that could not survive a design, and do not run a study that could not change a practice. Method first, vendor second.

## When this skill governs

Use at the start of any workplace people-question: engagement, hiring, leadership, burnout, "our culture," or a requested assessment.

## Core output

An **I-O Problem Frame**: problem in behaviour/outcome terms, stakeholders, method, ethics, what would falsify the favourite story.

## Phase 1 — Recode the complaint

"Low engagement" is not a diagnosis. Recode to observable: absenteeism, turnover, output, error, climate items, customer outcomes.

Name the population (role, site, contract type). Executives' theory of the problem is data, not truth.

## Phase 2 — Method menu

- **Experiment / A-B at work:** best for interventions; needs a unit and a holdout
- **Quasi-experiment:** staggered rollout, interrupted time series
- **Survey:** attitudes, climate; not performance itself
- **Archival HRIS:** turnover, absence, productivity — join carefully (HR tech course)
- **Qualitative:** interviews, critical incidents — for mechanism and job analysis, not as prevalence

Match: if they want to know whether a training works, a post-training smile sheet is the wrong method.

## Phase 3 — Ethics

Informed participation, power (employees cannot freely refuse), identifiable comments, high-stakes testing, deception. Anonymity vs confidentiality are different. Do not run a "survey" that is actually a performance trap without saying so.

## Phase 4 — Workflow

1. Recode to outcomes.
2. Pick method that could falsify.
3. Ethics screen.
4. Hand off to the matching I-O skill (selection, training, etc.).

## Anti-patterns

- Vendor assessment as the first move
- Survey as the only instrument
- Causal claims from one engagement pulse
- Ignoring employee power in "consent"

## Validation gate

Observable outcome; method could falsify; ethics named.

## Final principle

If the study could not have shown the favourite programme failed, it was not I-O science. It was internal marketing.
