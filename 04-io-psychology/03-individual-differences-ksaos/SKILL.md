# Individual Differences and KSAOs

## Mission

People differ. Not every difference is a job-relevant KSAO. Use constructs with evidence for this kind of work, and refuse fashionable typologies that do not predict performance.

## Core output

A **Predictor Relevance Note**: job (from job analysis), candidate constructs, evidence (validity, adverse impact), what not to measure.

## Phase 1 — Construct menu

- **GMA / cognitive ability:** often strongest general predictor of training and job performance, especially complex work; adverse impact risk
- **Job knowledge and skills:** high validity when well measured; closer to the work
- **Personality (Big Five):** conscientiousness is the most general; others are job-specific (extraversion in sales, etc.). Effects are smaller than GMA on average
- **Integrity / CWB-relevant traits:** relevant where counterproductive behaviour is costly
- **Interests and values:** fit and retention more than maximal performance
- **Physical abilities:** only when the job requires them

Not recommended as selection science: MBTI-type forced dichotomies, astrology, graphology, unvalidated "culture fit" chats.

## Phase 2 — Match to work

Complex problem-solving → cognitive + knowledge.  
Reliability, safety, follow-through → conscientiousness + integrity.  
Interdependent customer work → relevant interpersonal constructs measured behaviourally.

"Culture fit" that means "people like us" is a discrimination pathway. Fit to stated values/behaviours is a different, narrower claim.

## Phase 3 — Trade-offs

Validity vs adverse impact vs cost vs candidate experience. GMA-heavy systems often need alternative predictors or banding strategies if adverse impact is unacceptable (selection skill).

Do not stack 12 tests. Incremental validity beyond GMA + conscientiousness + a work sample is often small.

## Phase 4 — Workflow

1. Job-analytic KSAOs.
2. Map to constructs with evidence.
3. Drop low-relevance and junk measures.
4. Flag adverse-impact and faking risks.
5. Pass to measurement and selection skills.

## Anti-patterns

- MBTI for hiring
- Personality as the whole system
- Ignoring GMA where complexity is high because it is "unpopular"
- Culture-fit as similarity
- Medical or clinical constructs used as job tests without basis

## Validation gate

Job named; constructs linked to KSAOs; junk measures refused; adverse impact flagged.

## Final principle

Measure differences that the work uses. Everything else is curiosity, bias, or a vendor SKU.
