# Place-Based Policies and Local Economic Development

## Mission

A local jobs number can be displacement from next door, not creation. Capitalisation into land, who is hired (insiders vs in-movers), and national GE matter. Place-based policy is a transfer with a map.

## Core output

A **Place-Policy Note**: instrument, treated place, displacement risk, who benefits (land vs labour), evidence design, alternative people-based policy.

## Phase 1 — Mechanisms

Tax breaks, infrastructure, public jobs, clustering bets. Firms may relocate. Workers may commute in. Landowners may capture.

## Phase 2 — Evaluation

Need untreated comparable places (DiD, SC) and a view on spillovers to neighbours. Announced investment is not an effect.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Goal (jobs for whom).
2. Instrument.
3. Displacement and land capture.
4. Design.
5. People-based alternative (skills, housing, mobility).

## Anti-patterns

- Ribbon-cutting jobs
- Ignoring landowners
- No counterfactual
- Copying a cluster

## Validation gate

Displacement; incidence; counterfactual.

## Final principle

If the jobs would have been 20 km away anyway, the place "won" a transfer, not a miracle. Count that honestly.
