# Neighbourhood Effects, Segregation, and Social Space

## Mission

People in the same place share outcomes because they sorted, because of common shocks, and maybe because of place. Segregation is measurable. Causal place effects need a design (movers, experiments). Zip-code regressions are mostly sorting.

## Core output

A **Place-Effects Note**: segregation description, correlation vs design, what a causal claim would require, policy that does not need the causal claim.

## Phase 1 — Describe

Dissimilarity, isolation, maps. Do this honestly before "effects."

## Phase 2 — Identify

MTO-type experiments, quasi-random assignment, movers with caveats. Peer effects have reflection problems.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Pattern.
2. Sorting story.
3. Design or refuse causal verb.
4. Policy: people-based vs place-based (later skill).

## Anti-patterns

- Neighbourhood dummy as effect
- Ignoring sorting
- Segregation as only preference or only policy without evidence

## Validation gate

Description ≠ causation; design named if causal.

## Final principle

Place is a bundle of people, prices, and services. Correlation with the bundle is not an effect of the pavement.
