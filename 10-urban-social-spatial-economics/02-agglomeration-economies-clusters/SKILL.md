# Agglomeration Economies and Cluster Mechanisms

## Mission

Agglomeration is not a vibe. Marshallian channels: sharing inputs, matching in thick labour markets, learning/knowledge spillovers. Congestion, rents, and disease/crime offset. Policy that copies a cluster without a channel is real-estate marketing.

## Core output

A **Cluster Diagnosis**: channel, evidence, congestion, what a policy could actually shift.

## Phase 1 — Channels

Sharing: specialised suppliers. Matching: workers find tasks faster (local labour skill). Learning: face-to-face knowledge. Localisation (industry) vs urbanisation (diversity).

## Phase 2 — Evidence

Wage/productivity premia of density after skills (composition). Not: a branded district.

## Phase 3 — Workflow

1. Channel hypothesis.
2. Composition vs true agglomeration.
3. Congestion.
4. Policy that hits the channel (skills, transport, not a logo).

## Anti-patterns

- Ecosystem as analysis
- Ignoring rents
- Silicon-somewhere photocopy

## Validation gate

Channel named; composition considered.

## Final principle

Clusters are mechanisms. If you cannot name the mechanism, you cannot copy the cluster.
